About DFW Elite Mealpreps & Catering Co.

What started as a passion for meal prep has grown into something so much bigger.

DFW Elite Mealpreps & Catering Co. began with one simple goal: to make life easier for our clients through delicious, thoughtfully prepared food. What started in the meal prep world quickly grew into catering, weddings, private events, corporate gatherings, celebrations, and so much more — and we have loved every step of that journey.

Today, I’m incredibly proud to work alongside an amazing team that truly cares about the people we serve. Whether we’re preparing hundreds of weekly meal preps, setting up for a beautiful wedding, catering a corporate event, or helping a family celebrate one of life’s biggest moments, we bring the same level of care, attention, and heart to every experience.

For us, it has never been about simply serving food. It’s about serving people.

We believe every client deserves to feel heard, valued, and taken care of. Your goals matter to us. Your event matters to us. Your vision matters to us. We take the time to understand exactly what you are looking for and work alongside you to bring that vision to life in a way that feels personal, seamless, and memorable.

There is nothing we love more than watching an idea come together and seeing our clients enjoy the moment they trusted us to help create.

Whether it’s our next wedding, birthday party, corporate luncheon, private dinner, special celebration, or another meal prep adventure, our team is ready for it all.

We’re thankful for every client who has trusted us, supported our growth, recommended our business, and allowed us to be a small part of their lives.

At DFW Elite, you’re never just another order or another event on the calendar.

You come first — and we’re here to make your vision come to life.
